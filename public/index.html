Welcome, Detective

Welcome to the Murder Mystery Book Club at the University of Ottawa — where avid readers gather weekly to dissect clues, debate suspects, and celebrate the craft of crime fiction. Whether you're a seasoned Agatha Christie devotee or new to the genre, there's a seat at our table for you.

We meet every Monday in Room CRX 440-442. Check the Schedule for this week's book and time.

Announcements

    What We Do

    • Read one murder mystery novel per week, chosen by the group
    • Meet on Mondays for structured, guided discussion
    • Debate theories, motives, and plot twists together
    • Explore sub-genres: cozy mysteries, noir, psychological thrillers, and classic whodunits
    • Recommend reads and build a shared community reading list
    Learn More About the Club

    Join Us

    Membership is free and open to all University of Ottawa students, staff, and faculty. New members are always welcome — just show up on any Monday, or register in advance so we know to expect you.
