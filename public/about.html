About the Club

The Murder Mystery Book Club was founded in 2022 by a small group of University of Ottawa students who shared a passion for crime fiction and collaborative storytelling. What started as a study-break activity in a campus lounge has grown into a lively weekly gathering with members from all faculties.

Our club is dedicated to thoughtful, spoiler-rich discussion of murder mysteries in all their forms — from the golden-age classics of Agatha Christie and Dorothy Sayers to contemporary psychological thrillers and Nordic noir.

Our Mission

We aim to:

  • Foster a love of mystery and crime fiction on campus
  • Create a welcoming, inclusive space for readers of all experience levels
  • Encourage critical thinking through literary analysis
  • Introduce members to authors and sub-genres they might not have discovered on their own

How It Works

  1. A new book is announced each week via email and on this website.
  2. Members read the book before the Monday meeting.
  3. We meet in Room CRX 440-442 at the scheduled time.
  4. Discussion is guided but informal — every theory is welcome.
  5. At the end, we vote on the following week's selection.

Sub-Genres We Explore

Cozy Mysteries

Light-hearted and often set in small communities, cozy mysteries focus on amateur sleuths and downplay violence. Think village fêtes, eccentric neighbours, and impeccably timed revelations. Learn more at the Guardian Books - Crime Fiction section .

Noir & Hard-Boiled

Dark, atmospheric, and morally complex. Noir follows world-weary detectives through rain-soaked cities and corrupt institutions. Raymond Chandler and Dashiell Hammett are essential reading.

Psychological Thrillers

Unreliable narrators, twisting timelines, and deep dives into motive and obsession. Modern authors like Tana French, Gillian Flynn, and Ruth Ware dominate this space.

Historical Mysteries

Period settings add an extra layer of intrigue. From Victorian London to ancient Rome, historical mysteries blend detective fiction with immersive world-building.
